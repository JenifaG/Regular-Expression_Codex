When the market scanner needs a bigger grammar

Regex describes regular languages and is great for local text shapes. Context-free grammars (CFGs) handle recursive nesting, such as balanced parentheses. A parser uses a stack to match arbitrarily deep pairs; a plain regular expression has no stack.

S → (S)S | ε

This CFG can describe (), (()), and ()().
